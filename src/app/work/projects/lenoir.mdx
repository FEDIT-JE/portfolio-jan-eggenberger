Led the development of a visually striking website for Le Noir Web, a creative digital agency specializing in high-end web design and branding. The project focused on creating an elegant, user-friendly, and responsive website that showcases their work and services.

- **Branding & Identity**: Development of strong visual identities, including logos, color schemes, and typography, to reflect a brand's essence.
- **UX/UI**: Crafting intuitive user experiences with clean and functional interfaces to ensure ease of use and high conversion rates.
- **Web Hosting & Maintenance**: Offering continuous web hosting, security, and maintenance to ensure optimal performance and uptime.
